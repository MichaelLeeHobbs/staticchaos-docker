# Patryn QoL — runestats output, defenses toggle (#7)

GitHub issue: https://github.com/MichaelLeeHobbs/staticchaos-docker/issues/7 · Epic: #1

Mostly clarity/feedback, not balance. Makes a Patryn's defensive state legible.

## Implemented (`src/classes/patryn.c`)
1. **`runestats` defensive summary** — after the raw rune list, adds:
   - **Defenses: raised / lowered**.
   - **Torso runes** (Earth/Fire/Air/Water) — note: earth+fire drive damage mitigation.
   - **Arm runes** — Fire (melee damage) / Earth (parry).
   - **Leg runes** — Earth (reduces enemy parry/dodge/block).
   - **Active block(s)** (air/fire/negative) and **active ward(s)** with **remaining duration**
     (new `runestats_ward` helper), priority spirit/earth/flame/wind/water/negative.
2. **`defenses raise/lower`** — adds `WAIT_STATE` (4 on raise, 2 on lower) and clearer status
   text ("...the tattoos across your body harden with light" / "...power flow back into your
   hands"). Existing +15% mana / −10% melee penalties while raised are unchanged.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] `runestats` shows defenses state, rune layout with labels, active blocks, and active wards
      with tick counts — all from live data.
- [ ] `defenses raise`/`lower` impose a short wait and print the new status text.

## Deferred (documented)
- **Exact derived magnitudes** in `runestats` (mitigation %, fire-arm melee %, earth-arm parry +,
  earth-leg break −): these are formula-driven across several fight.c sites; shown here as their
  rune *inputs* with what each drives. Mirroring the exact numbers is a follow-up so the displayed
  figures can't drift from the real combat math.
- **Ward-absorb feedback messages**: the absorb logic is spread across ~13 sites in fight.c's
  damage paths; adding per-hit messages there is a separate, careful change — deferred.

## Risk & balance notes
- Display + a small WAIT_STATE only; no combat-math change. Numbers (wait 4/2) tunable.
